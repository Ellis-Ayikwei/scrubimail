Frequently Asked Questions

01. How accurate is the validation?

SCRUBI achieves a 99.98% precision rate by utilizing a multi-layered verification stack including SMTP handshake simulation, syntax analysis, and proprietary spam-trap fingerprints. We don't just guess; we verify against live servers.

02. Do you support custom integrations?

Yes. Beyond our standard REST API, we offer Webhooks, gRPC streams for ultra-low latency, and native SDKs for Node.js, Go, and Python. Enterprise customers can request bespoke connectors for legacy systems.

03. What's the sub-500ms guarantee?

Our global edge network ensures that validation requests are processed at the node nearest to your server. We guarantee that 95% of individual validation requests will return a status in under 500ms, or we credit your account.
